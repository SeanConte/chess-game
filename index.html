The Game

This chess variant involves outnumbering positions. There is no attacking — pieces are captured when their position is outnumbered. Players start with banks of pieces which they place on the board to gain territorial advantage. The game features the following modes & options:

  • Game Mode:
    • Sudden Death: If any of a player's pieces are outnumbered at the end of their turn, they lose the game.
    • Queens Gambit: Pieces left outnumbered at the end of a player's turn are removed, but the game ends only when their last queen (board + bank) is gone.
    • Total War: Pieces left outnumbered are removed. A player loses only when they have zero total pieces (board + bank).
  • Movement:
    • None: Once a piece is placed, it never moves. (If the game doesn't end before both banks are empty, the player with the most pieces remaining on the board wins.)
    • Line of sight: Instead of placing a new piece, players can move a piece already on the board according to where it can see (attack, under normal rules of chess) excluding spots that are already outnumbered or occupied.
    • Anywhere: Instead of placing a new piece, players can teleport one of their already placed pieces to any spot not already occupied or outnumbered.
  • Starting Armies: Choose piece banks to start with.
  • AI: Enable an AI to play as Black. The depth indicates how many moves ahead it will consider. The style indicates what board considerations it favors.
  • Timed games: classic chess rules — players must each keep total playing time below some limit (60 seconds, 5 minutes, unlimited)
  • Pause between turns: briefly pause after each move — useful for sharing the same device during timed games
  • Heatmap: tint squares by control (your control vs opponent) with intensity based on overlapping coverage.
